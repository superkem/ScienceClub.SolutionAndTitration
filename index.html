Science Club · 40-minute bench protocol

Solution preparation, dilution and acid–base titration

Make one shared baking soda stock, dilute vinegar tenfold, then neutralise one with the other. You do the calculations. The page checks them, shows each technique step by step and lets you rehearse the reaction before you pour.

      18–33 min · Activity 3

      Acid–base reaction challenge

      Add baking soda stock to diluted vinegar a little at a time and compare how the mixture changes. The goal is to compare the changes as more base is added, not to find an exact equivalence point.

      Goggles on. Do not seal the cup. The reaction releases gas and keep solutions away from your eyes.
